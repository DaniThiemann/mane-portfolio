<f. maeda keiei>

> brand identity
> web design

> f. maeda keiei structures international expansion for consumer brands moving into europe and latam. their argument is that expansion rarely fails from lack of ambition — it fails from bad structure. so the identity had to perform discipline rather than describe it.

 

> five circles hold a pentagon: the most stable arrangement that still reads as separate parts. black and white only — no greys, no tints — because their decisions are binary too. the wordmark carries the whole hierarchy in a single move, "f. maeda" bold against "keiei" light.

 

> the site is built around a bonsai — growth that is deliberate, constrained and slow. copy arrives out of focus and resolves as you scroll, and the method cascades down the page one step at a time. clarity before scale, made literal.

<z-hive>

> brand identity
> web design
> ux/ui

> z-hive installs home automation that runs entirely inside the house — no cloud, no subscriptions, no latency. the promise is a home that responds instantly and then gets out of the way, so the design had to feel calm rather than technological.

 

> the mark is one continuous ribbon folding back on itself: a single path routing through a structure, which is exactly what the system is. it survives any scale, from a 4mm glass switch to an illuminated concrete facade.

 

> the site is called rotina em fluxo. an isometric blueprint maps the house as one hub and its satellites, with light pulsing along the connections; a scene simulator lets you switch between morning, cinema and night and watch the room answer. cool signal-white belongs to the system, warm amber belongs to the home — the two temperatures never mix.
